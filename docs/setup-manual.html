Before you start

Have these ready. The whole setup takes about ten minutes, or about two minutes per site after the first.

  • A work email address you will keep. It is how you sign in and where the sign-in link goes. There is no password.
  • The premises address and postcode for each site you want to track.
  • The responsible person for each site — the name and email of whoever signs the logbook off and receives the reminders.
  • The days that site is open. Monday to Friday is assumed; change it if the site opens at weekends or closes midweek.

The one rule to remember One firm per business, not one firm per person. Colleagues should never sign in for the first time with their own address — that creates a separate firm with its own sites and billing. Invite them instead (step 6).
1

Create your firm

Signing in for the first time creates your user, your firm and your owner membership in one step. You do not fill in a company registration form or wait for approval.

  1. Open firelogbook.vercel.app/signin.
  2. Enter your work email and choose Email me a sign-in link.
  3. Open the email and click the link. It expires after 20 minutes and works once.

What gets created

  • Your firm, named from your email domain — jo@marshvale.co.uk becomes “Marshvale”. You can rename it during onboarding or later in Settings.
  • Your membership as the owner. Owners can invite people, edit every site and manage billing.
  • A 14-day trial with full access. No card is taken.
Already have an account? Signing in with an address that belongs to an existing firm takes you straight back to that firm. A first-time address always starts a new one.
2

Add your first site

With no sites on the account, sign-in lands on Add your first site. This form creates the site and builds your first audit pack in the same step.

Business name *
Your firm's name as it should appear on audit packs and emails. Change it here if the name taken from your email domain is wrong.
Site name *
How you will recognise this premises in the list — “Harbour Lane Dental”, not “Site 1”.
Address
Street address for the premises. Appears on the audit pack cover.
Postcode
Stored in capitals. Useful when an insurer or inspector asks which premises a record belongs to.
Responsible person
Who signs the logbook off. This is the name printed on the audit pack and the first person reminders chase.
Their email
Where the weekly reminders go. It can be your own address. Reminders are only as good as this field.

Choose Create logbook and build first audit pack. You land on the new site's audit pack, dated and ready to share even before the first check is recorded.

Common mistake Leaving the responsible person's email blank or mistyped. A bounced reminder is the same as no reminder — check this field on every site after setup.
3

Add the other sites

Once a site exists, the guided form stops appearing. Add the rest from the dashboard.

  1. Go to Dashboard.
  2. Choose Add Site in the top right.
  3. Fill in the site name and responsible person, then choose Create Site.

The simpler form asks for the site name, address, postcode, and the responsible person's name and email. New sites start with Monday to Friday working days, reminders switched on, and the thirteen-check checklist already in place.

One site per premises

Create a site for each physical address, not for each department or each person. Two buildings with separate alarm systems are two sites. A single building with three floors is one site.

Adding sites after the trial The dashboard's Add Site needs an active subscription. Sites you already have stay readable if the trial ends without subscribing, but adding new ones is paused until billing starts.
Site limit The guided first-site form accepts up to 25 sites on one firm. If you are setting up more than that, add them from the dashboard and contact the operator about a multi-site account.
4

Edit site details

Open any site and choose Edit site details. Owners and administrators can change:

  • Site name, address and postcode
  • Responsible person and their reminder email
  • Working days, which decide when weekly and monthly checks fall due

Choose Save and the page refreshes with the new values. Records already stored are untouched — editing details changes the site, not its history.

When a responsible person leaves Change the name and email on the site that same week. Their old address keeps receiving reminders until you do, and a new person will not be chased at all.
5

Turn on reminders

Reminders are on by default. Open Reminders to confirm the schedule and see exactly what would be sent now.

Weekly reminders
The master switch. When it is off, nothing is sent for any site.
Send on
The day of the week for the pass, Monday by default.
Send after
A UK time between 06:00 and 10:00. The daily run happens at 10:00 UTC, so this is the earliest a message can go out, not an exact delivery time.

One message goes per site per week, at the highest level reached. Reminders stop for a site when someone uses the unsubscribe link in one of its emails; the site page then shows reminders off.

If reminders are not arriving Check four things in this order: the site's reminder email, the master switch, the send day, and whether the site was opted out. Then check the recipient's spam folder.
6

Invite your team

Only the owner can invite people. Go to Settings, then Team Management, then Manage Team Members.

  • Admin — can edit site details and record checks, but cannot invite people.
  • Member — can record checks and view the logbook.
  • Owner — everything, including team and billing. The person who created the firm starts as owner.

Enter the address, pick a role and choose Invite. The invitation is valid for seven days. Opening the link signs the person in and adds them to your firm's sites; they do not need to set a password or create a firm.

If the email does not arrive Check the address and the recipient's spam folder, then send another invitation. Each invitation creates a new seven-day link; the earlier one stays valid until it expires or is used.
Names on records Each check is stamped with the signed-in person's name. Set your name in Settings first, otherwise the email address is used instead.
7

Trial and billing

The trial runs for 14 days from your first sign-in and includes every feature. Nothing is charged and no card is needed to start.

£29 per site, per month
The monthly plan. The quantity on the subscription follows the number of sites on the account.
£290 per site, per year
The annual plan, equivalent to two months free.
Adding or removing sites
Billing follows the site count. Add a site and the per-site charge applies; remove one and it comes off.

Open Billing to start a subscription or manage an existing one through Stripe. Starting a subscription before the trial ends saves the card immediately but does not take the first payment until the trial would have finished.

If the trial ends without a subscription The account becomes read-only. Your sites, records, photos and audit packs stay in place and become fully available again as soon as a subscription starts. Nothing is deleted.

Troubleshooting

SymptomWhat to do
“That sign-in link has expired”Links last 20 minutes and work once. Request a new one. If a second request is refused, wait a minute and try again.
Signed in, but the sites are missingYou signed in with a different address, which belongs to a different firm. Sign out and use the original email.
A colleague cannot see the sitesThey signed in instead of being invited, so they have their own firm. Invite them from Settings and ask them to open the emailed link.
Reminders never arriveCheck the site's reminder email, the master switch, the send day and any opt-out. Ask the recipient to check spam.
“Read-only” appears in the headerThe trial has ended without a subscription. Open Billing to start one; records are untouched.
The Add Site button does not workAdding sites needs an active subscription. Existing sites remain readable.
The firm name is wrongSettings, then Firm Settings, then Firm Name. It appears on audit packs and emails.
Reminders go to the wrong personOpen the site, choose Edit site details, and change the responsible person and email together.
